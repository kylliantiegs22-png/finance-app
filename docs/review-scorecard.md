# Persona review scorecard

Used by the persona playtest goal (`PERSONA_REVIEW_GOAL_PROMPT.md`). Each persona plays through the app and scores it here. Results go in `docs/persona-review.md`.

**Key objective:** assess educational effectiveness, usability, functionality, accessibility and overall app quality, with the most weight on whether the persona actually learns something they can use in their own life this week.

## Scorecard (100 points)

| Category | Points | What a top score means for this app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Educational value & learning outcomes | 20 | The persona can do each lesson's "By the end you can" (see `docs/curriculum.md`) without the app, and can name one thing they will do differently with their own money. |
| Content accuracy & quality | 15 | Australian figures, terms and examples are correct and current; explanations are clear at this persona's reading level; examples feel like their life, not someone else's. |
| User experience & navigation | 15 | Onboarding, path, lesson, feedback, recap, review and unit check are obvious with no dead ends or "what now?" moments. |
| Functionality & reliability | 15 | Everything works: answers mark correctly, progress saves, streak and review behave, reload and offline keep progress, no console errors. |
| Student engagement & interactivity | 10 | The persona wants to come back tomorrow: habit plan, reminder, forgiving streak, money missions and feedback feel motivating, not nagging. |
| Accessibility & inclusivity | 10 | Works by keyboard and screen reader, readable contrast, reduced motion respected; examples include people of different ages, incomes and family types without judgement. |
| Visual design & presentation | 5 | Clean, consistent and calm on a 390x844 phone, light and dark. |
| Performance & responsiveness | 5 | Fast first load and instant screen changes on a mid-range phone. |
| Privacy & data security | 5 | No accounts, tracking or data leaving the device; the persona would trust it. |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## Rating scale

- 90–100: Outstanding
- 80–89: Excellent
- 70–79: Good
- 60–69: Satisfactory
- Below 60: Needs improvement

## Reviewer feedback (every persona, every round)

- **Strengths:** what works well?
- **Weaknesses:** what needs improvement?
- **Recommendations:** what should be developed further?
- **Real-life test:** after the unit, the persona writes their own short plan using only what the app taught (for example, a weekly budget or a savings goal with numbers). Note any step they could not do and which lesson should have taught it.

## The five personas

| Persona | Life | What they want from the app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emma, 16 | High school student, pocket money and maybe a casual job | Save pocket money and manage spending |
| Jake, 20 | University student | Budget for rent, groceries, transport and study costs |
| Olivia, 24 | Graduate nurse | Understand payslips, tax, savings and super |
| Liam, 28 | Young professional | Investing, managing debt, buying a first home |
| Sarah, 35 | Single parent | Household expenses, reducing debt, an emergency fund |

Score what the app has now. Topics that belong to a later unit (for example tax, super, investing or home loans) are noted under Recommendations, not marked down, as long as the app is honest about what is coming and the current unit still helps that persona start.
